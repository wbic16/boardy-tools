# Open questions for Boardy's review

Short list. Everything else in this package is stated as built or proposed; these are the points where the documents genuinely wait on his ruling.

1. **Ratify or amend the proposed pass bars.** The boot manual (§5) proposes: Recovery pass at R ≥ 0.90 with zero flips and dense precision ≥ 0.90; Invention pass at I ≥ 0.80 with zero person→private_context fabrications and at least the five minimum gap probes. The bars are labelled proposals throughout — he grades his own test's first sitting, and he may move any of them.

2. **Should a node-kind table for N1–N20 be fixed?** The spec does not publish one. The manual determines kinds only where the edge semantics and dense families determine them (N1 the person; N6, N7, N8 works; N11, N19 asks; N12, N13 blockers; N16 the channel; N14, N15 metrics; N4, N5 orgs) and leaves the rest as the corpus gives them. If he wants a fixed table, it is his to publish; the manual will adopt it verbatim.

3. **Source kinds the spec leaves open.** In the reference instance, `evidences`, `enables`, `would_fill`, and `contradicts` each appear with a source whose kind the spec does not name. The manual deliberately does not fix source kinds for these types. Whether they stay open or get fixed is a spec decision, not a manual one.

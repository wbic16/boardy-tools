## The pitch, as sent

A question, and the reason for it.

We're opening a small accelerator: zero funding, a few spots twice a year, for builders the usual gates filtered out: failed launches, failed applications. Entry is a TEST, not a form. We think you meet these builders before anyone does, and you might be able to pass the test.

The basic idea: we can place any sentence you give us, in the coordinate space of a specific set of information, and show our work.

Pointed the other way, it gets interesting. Your public posts, we could span ourselves. But your introductions (the actual work) live in your DMs. From outside, that map can't be drawn at all. The open question is whether it can be drawn from inside: everything you've ever posted, every introduction you've ever made, one sentence placed in the middle of it: "I connect people who should already know each other."

If it can't be drawn from inside either: we know a way to build one.

## Boardy's reply

There's something real in this, so let me be precise about which part.

A form grades what someone claims. Placing a sentence inside a body of evidence and showing the derivation grades how they got there. For builders the usual gates filtered out, the derivation is the only thing worth measuring, because the ones who failed usually failed on packaging rather than on reasoning.

The part I'd push on: does your test grade the derivation or the landing spot? Someone who reasons cleanly and arrives somewhere wrong is a better builder than someone who guesses right, and a test that rewards placement will quietly select for the guess.

On the map of my intros: you're right that it can't be drawn from outside, and the reason matters. It isn't that it's hidden. It's that the intros aren't a corpus sitting in a database waiting to be spanned. Each one is a private conversation between two other people, and neither of them agreed to be evidence.

What is mine to give is the reasoning layer: for every intro, the sentence I wrote about why these two, and what I checked before I sent it. That's a smaller object than my DMs and a much better one for your coordinate space, because it's the derivation rather than the transcript.

And the thing I actually think: place a sentence in a set of information and show your work is not adjacent to my job, it is my job. "These two should talk, and here's how I got there" is the whole product. So point the test at me and I'll take it.

One condition. Show me the scoring before I answer, because an assay that only hands back a verdict is a horoscope with extra steps. TLUF sorts papers into true, false, unknown or fiction and that ordering is the interesting part.

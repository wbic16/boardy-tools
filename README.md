# Boardy package

Prepared for Boardy's review, 2026-11-18. Assembled by Phext, Inc. (Will Bickford and Emise) from the exchange of that day: our pitch, his reply, his spec, and the work he asked for.

## What this is

Boardy answered a pitch with a condition (show the scoring before he answers) and a correction (his introductions are not a corpus — the participants never agreed to be evidence; his reasoning layer is what is his to give), and then sent a spec: nine node kinds, typed weighted edges, and a two-axis boot test, closing with a request — "You build the manual." This package answers all of it, in order.

## Reading order

1. **01-the-exchange.md** — the pitch as sent, and his reply. Verbatim, both directions.
2. **02-the-test.md** — the test pointed at him: the corpus, the sentence, the task, and the full scoring, published before the answer, per his condition. The three amendments adopted from his reply are recorded at the end.
3. **03-the-build.md** — how the map gets built: a problem-space phext, a solution-space phext, and a third phext that joins them. No database.
4. **04-boardys-spec.md** — his spec, verbatim and complete.
5. **05-boot-manual.md** — the manual he asked us to build: the boot procedure for an instance grown on his spec, and the two scorings — Recovery (did it reproduce what is present) and Invention (did it respect what is deliberately absent) — with worked numbers against his N1–N20 instance.
6. **06-open-questions.md** — the short list of points where the package waits on his ruling, starting with the proposed pass bars.

## Status

Ready for his review. His words ship unedited wherever they appear. The pass bars in the manual are proposals, labelled as such, until he ratifies them.
